Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty
[One Line, One Chill](https://make.fun/c/u-azazex-one-line-one-chill-6d4d5488).

One sentence of horror (166 characters, under the 200 limit). The scare is the twist: the voice on the baby monitor is the narrator's own six-year-old voice — and the intruder in the bed already knows the child is awake.

`main.py` (Python 3) writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`:

> The baby monitor crackled, and in my own voice — sleepy, small, six years old — something whispered, 'Mummy, the man in your bed isn't Daddy, and he knows I'm awake.'

Run it locally with:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
